# Human cerebellar organoids with functional Purkinje cells

## Source

- DOI: [10.1016/j.stem.2023.11.013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stem.2023.11.013)
- PMID: 38181749
- Raw PDF: [source](../../raw/sources/20260718_233522_atamian-2024-human-cerebellar-organoids-with-functional.pdf)

## Key findings

- Human cerebellar organoids reproducibly generated major cerebellar neuronal and supporting populations, including roof plate, choroid plexus, rhombic-lip lineages, interneurons, and Purkinje cells.
- The system produced a human-specific rhombic-lip subventricular-zone progenitor population relevant to cerebellar development and medulloblastoma hypotheses.
- Organoids transiently formed laminar organization and generated functionally active neurons, including Purkinje cells.
- Six-month organoid Purkinje cells resembled approximately 22–28 post-conception-week human fetal Purkinje cells, defining a developmental ceiling rather than adult maturity.

## Relevance

- Expands [brain-subregion-specific organoid protocols](../concepts/brain-subregion-specific-organoid-protocols.md) beyond cortical identity to a multi-lineage cerebellar system with functional readouts.

## Limitations

- Lamination was transient and maturation remained fetal-like.
- Similarity to fetal references and electrophysiological activity do not establish a complete cerebellar circuit.
