# Multi-zonal liver organoids from human pluripotent stem cells

## Source

- DOI: [10.1038/s41586-025-08850-1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08850-1)
- PMID: 40240593
- Raw PDF: [source](../../raw/sources/20260718_233522_al-reza-2025-multi-zonal-liver-organoids-from-human.pdf)

## Key findings

- Ascorbate- and bilirubin-conditioned hiPSC hepatic progenitors self-assembled into organoids containing periportal, interzonal, and pericentral hepatocyte-like populations.
- Conditioned cells showed zone-associated functions involving urea-cycle, glutathione, and glutamate metabolism.
- snRNA-seq reconstructed a hepatoblast-to-zonal-hepatocyte trajectory; epigenetic analyses implicated ascorbate-dependent EP300–TET1 and bilirubin-dependent EP300–HIF1α mechanisms.
- Transplantation improved survival and hyperammonaemia/hyperbilirubinaemia in bile-duct-ligated immunodeficient rats.

## Relevance

- Shows that organoid regionalization can encode metabolic division of labour, not only anatomical marker differences.
- Extends [gastrointestinal and endodermal organoid systems](../concepts/gastrointestinal-and-endodermal-organoid-systems.md) with a zonation-first design and transplantation validation.

## Limitations

- Organoid zonal cells retained discrepancies from primary liver, including immature marker expression.
- Rat rescue after transplantation does not establish full human liver zonal architecture or long-term safety.
