# Single-cell transcriptomic comparison of tubular segment maturation in advanced human in vitro kidney models

## Source

- DOI: [10.1016/j.isci.2026.116053](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2026.116053)
- PMID: 42231968
- Raw PDF: [source](../../raw/sources/20260718_233522_pou-casellas-2026-single-cell-transcriptomic-comparison-of-tubular.pdf)

## Key findings

- Integrated scRNA-seq benchmarked tissue-derived tubuloids, iPSC-organoid-derived tubuloids, and four iPSC kidney-organoid protocols against fetal and adult human nephron references.
- Models differed in tubular cell identity, adaptive/injury-like states, and maturation-marker expression rather than forming a single quality hierarchy.
- iPSC organoid-derived tubuloids reduced some immature and off-target populations while retaining a hybrid model identity.
- Distal tubule, connecting tubule, and intercalated cells remained scarce across the included systems.

## Relevance

- Converts [kidney organoid differentiation routes](../concepts/kidney-organoid-differentiation-routes.md) into a model-selection problem grounded in cell-state composition and intended use.
- Supports reference-atlas benchmarking before interpreting injury, transport, or maturation phenotypes.

## Limitations

- Cross-study integration inherits protocol, donor, batch, and analysis differences from source datasets.
- Transcriptomic maturity does not directly establish transport, filtration, or injury-response function.
