# Tissue culture models of AKI: from tubule cells to human kidney organoids

> Secondary source: this is a review and does not report a new experimental organoid dataset.

## Source

- DOI: [10.1681/ASN.2021050693](https://doi.org/10.1681/ASN.2021050693)
- PMID: 35031569
- Raw PDF: [source](../../raw/sources/20260718_233522_bejoy-2022-tissue-culture-models-of-aki.pdf)

## Synthesis

- Compares immortalized and primary tubule-cell systems, hPSC-derived renal cells, kidney organoids, bioprinting, and kidney-on-chip approaches for acute kidney injury research.
- Kidney organoid injury can expose multicellular crosstalk and biomarkers that are difficult to reproduce in simple tubule cultures.
- Major organoid limitations are fetal-like immaturity, off-target populations, incomplete nephron segments, and limited transport/flow context.
- The review frames model choice as a trade-off between throughput and mechanistic simplicity versus tissue diversity and physiological context.

## Relevance

- Provides a model-selection scaffold for [kidney organoid differentiation routes](../concepts/kidney-organoid-differentiation-routes.md), but individual mechanistic claims should be traced to cited primary studies.
