# Overview

This collection covers neurodevelopmental genetics, variant interpretation, functional genomics, and cellular or circuit references relevant to ASD and related neurodevelopmental disorders. It contains a curated deep-ingested manuscript-reference set alongside a larger metadata-only backlog.

## Current deep anchors

- Human genetic evidence: rare coding variation, CNVs, mosaicism, regulatory variation, and gene discovery.
- Functional evidence: saturation mutagenesis, variant-effect maps, and PS3/BS3 calibration.
- Developmental references: [early human brain single-cell states and fetal cortical spatial organization](concepts/human-fetal-brain-reference-atlases.md).
- Circuit mechanisms: [cell-type-specific and non-cell-autonomous effects of NDD genes](concepts/ndd-genes-in-cell-type-and-circuit-context.md).

## New 2026-07-18 contribution

- Eze 2021 provides a first-trimester primary human reference and identifies stage-dependent limits of organoid fidelity.
- Qian 2025 adds spatial layer and areal specification across more than 18 million fetal cortical cells.
- Anderson 2020 shows how Foxp1 perturbation differs across striatal projection-neuron classes and propagates non-cell-autonomously in a mouse circuit.

## Interpretation rules

- Do not transfer mouse cell/circuit mechanisms directly to human disease without human validation.
- Match any model to a primary developmental reference at the relevant region and stage.
- Distinguish direct perturbation effects from compensatory responses and descriptive atlas associations from causal evidence.
- Treat queued metadata pages as backlog, not as fully supported knowledge.
