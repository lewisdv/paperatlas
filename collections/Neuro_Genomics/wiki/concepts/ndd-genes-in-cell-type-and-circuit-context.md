# NDD genes in cell-type and circuit context

## Current position

An NDD-associated gene can regulate different programs across cell types, and perturbing one lineage can trigger compensatory responses in others. Gene-level association therefore does not define one universal mechanism.

## Anchor source

- [Anderson 2020](../sources/20260718_234948_anderson-2020-single-cell-analysis-of-foxp1-driven-mechanisms.md) separates Foxp1 effects in direct- and indirect-pathway striatal projection neurons and detects non-cell-autonomous changes across the circuit.

## Working rules

- Specify species, developmental stage, cell type, and circuit before interpreting an NDD-gene phenotype.
- Distinguish direct perturbation targets from secondary compensation.
- Require human validation before transferring mouse circuit mechanisms to human disease.
