# Spatial transcriptomics reveals human cortical layer and area specification

## Key findings

- MERFISH with deep-learning nucleus segmentation generated a spatial atlas of more than 18 million cells across eight human fetal cortical areas and seven developmental time points.
- Excitatory-neuron subtype distributions revealed a molecular six-layer structure approximately three months before cytoarchitectural layers became apparent.
- Most cortical areas showed continuous anterior–posterior transitions, but the V1–V2 visual-cortex boundary was discrete by gestational week 20.
- Integration with snRNA-seq found early synaptogenesis upregulation in V1-specific layer-4 neurons.

## Methods

- MERFISH targeted spatial profiling, custom nucleus segmentation, hierarchical cell typing, snRNA-seq integration, Visium/in-situ whole-transcriptome support, and cross-platform validation.

## Interpretation and limitations

- The coexistence of gradient-like and sharp areal boundaries argues against a single uniform model of mid-gestation cortical arealization.
- MERFISH uses a selected gene panel, segmentation and imputation are computational steps, and fetal tissue is cross-sectional rather than longitudinal.
