# A spatially resolved multiomic single-cell atlas of soybean development

## Key findings

- Integrated single-cell gene expression, chromatin accessibility, and spatial transcriptomics across ten soybean tissues identified 103 cell types and 303,199 accessible chromatin regions.
- Nearly 40% of accessible regions were cell-type specific and enriched for transcription-factor motifs associated with cellular identity.
- Regulatory-network analysis addressed legume symbiotic nitrogen fixation and reconstructed embryo and endosperm developmental trajectories.
- Late peripheral endosperm co-upregulated 13 sucrose transporters sharing a DOF11 motif; embryo analysis nominated regulators of cotyledon parenchyma identity.

## Relevance and limitations

- A clear example of using spatial position to interpret matched expression–accessibility regulatory programs rather than treating modalities as parallel atlases.
- Regulatory links and motif enrichment are hypotheses unless supported by perturbation; cross-tissue integration also inherits dissociation and batch effects.
