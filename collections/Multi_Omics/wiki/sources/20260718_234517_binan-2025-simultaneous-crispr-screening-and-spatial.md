# Simultaneous CRISPR screening and spatial transcriptomics reveal intracellular, intercellular, and functional transcriptional circuits

## Key findings

- Perturb-FISH combines imaging spatial transcriptomics with optical detection of in-situ-amplified guide RNAs, assigning perturbation and molecular state in spatially resolved cells.
- In LPS-stimulated monocytes, it reproduced intracellular effects seen by Perturb-seq while uncovering density-dependent and intercellular innate-immune regulation.
- In 3D xenografts, the method identified genetic perturbations that altered tumor–immune interactions.
- A separate hiPSC-astrocyte screen linked autism-risk-gene perturbations to calcium phenotypes, genetic interactions, and dysregulated pathways.

## Relevance and limitations

- The method closes a causal gap between spatial association and perturbation while allowing an independent functional readout.
- Targeted transcript panels, imaging throughput, guide assignment, and context-specific screens limit generalization; hits require independent validation.
