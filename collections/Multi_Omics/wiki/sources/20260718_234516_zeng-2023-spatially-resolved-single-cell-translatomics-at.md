# Spatially resolved single-cell translatomics at molecular resolution

## Key findings

- RIBOmap uses rRNA/mRNA tri-probes, in-situ amplification, hydrogel embedding, and barcode sequencing to map ribosome-bound mRNAs in three dimensions.
- A 981-gene HeLa-cell experiment detected cell-cycle-dependent and subcellularly localized translation.
- In mouse brain, 5,413 genes were profiled across 119,173 cells, revealing cell-type- and region-specific translational regulation and remodeling during oligodendrocyte maturation.
- Paired spatial transcriptomic/translatomic maps expose regulation that cannot be inferred reliably from mRNA abundance alone.

## Limitations

- The method is targeted rather than unbiased and detects ribosome association as a proxy for active translation.
- Probe design, tissue accessibility, and in-situ sequencing errors constrain scale and comparability.
