# A single-cell atlas of chromatin accessibility in the human genome

## Key findings

- Single-cell chromatin-accessibility profiles from 30 adult tissue types were integrated with 15 fetal tissue types.
- The atlas covered more than 1.3 million nuclei, 222 cell types, and approximately 1.2 million candidate cis-regulatory elements.
- Cell-type-specific accessibility maps linked regulatory elements to developmental stage and tissue context.
- The resource supports interpretation of noncoding variants associated with complex traits and diseases by narrowing candidate regulatory cell types.

## Limitations

- Accessibility is potential regulatory activity, not direct evidence that an element controls a nominated gene.
- Donor and tissue sampling remain uneven, and variant-to-function interpretation requires orthogonal or perturbational validation.
