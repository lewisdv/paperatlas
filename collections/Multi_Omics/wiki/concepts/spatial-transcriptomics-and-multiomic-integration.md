# Spatial transcriptomics and multiomic integration

## Current position

Spatial multi-omics is most informative when location changes the interpretation of cell state or regulatory activity, not when spatial data are merely displayed beside single-cell clusters.

## Evidence in this collection

- [Soybean spatial multiomic atlas](../sources/20260718_234516_zhang-2024-a-spatially-resolved-multiomic-single-cell.md) links expression, chromatin accessibility, developmental trajectories, and tissue location.
- [Spotiphy](../sources/20260718_234516_yang-2025-spotiphy-enables-single-cell-spatial-whole.md) computationally bridges spot-level whole-transcriptome coverage and single-cell histological resolution.
- [RIBOmap](../sources/20260718_234516_zeng-2023-spatially-resolved-single-cell-translatomics-at.md) adds spatial translation to reveal post-transcriptional regulation.
- [Perturb-FISH](../sources/20260718_234517_binan-2025-simultaneous-crispr-screening-and-spatial.md) adds causal perturbation and functional readouts.

## Evidence ladder

1. Map spatial cell states.
2. Integrate regulatory or translational modalities.
3. Distinguish measured from computationally inferred resolution.
4. Perturb candidate regulators and validate an independent phenotype.

## Main tension

- whole-transcriptome coverage versus direct single-cell measurement
- descriptive spatial association versus causal spatial circuitry
