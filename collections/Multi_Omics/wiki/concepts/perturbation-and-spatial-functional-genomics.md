# Perturbation and spatial functional genomics

## Current position

Spatial association becomes mechanistically stronger when each cell carries a known perturbation and an independent functional phenotype can be measured.

## Anchor source

- [Binan 2025 Perturb-FISH](../sources/20260718_234517_binan-2025-simultaneous-crispr-screening-and-spatial.md) combines guide detection, spatial transcriptomics, and functional assays across monocytes, xenografts, and hiPSC astrocytes.

## Working synthesis

- Intracellular effects can be compared with Perturb-seq-like expectations.
- Spatial context reveals density dependence and cell–cell interactions hidden by dissociation.
- A separate phenotype such as calcium activity prevents transcriptomic rescue from being mistaken for functional rescue.

## Main limitations

Target panels, guide efficiency, optical crowding, and context-specific cell models constrain coverage. Screen hits remain hypotheses until independently reproduced.
