# Spatial translatomics and post-transcriptional regulation

## Current position

mRNA abundance is an incomplete proxy for protein production. Spatial translatomics measures ribosome-associated transcripts while preserving cell and tissue context.

## Anchor source

- [Zeng 2023 RIBOmap](../sources/20260718_234516_zeng-2023-spatially-resolved-single-cell-translatomics-at.md) mapped 5,413 ribosome-bound transcripts in 119,173 mouse-brain cells and identified regional, cell-type, maturation, and subcellular translation patterns.

## Interpretation rule

Compare spatial transcriptome and translatome within matched tissue. Treat ribosome association as evidence of translational engagement, not direct protein abundance, and validate key targets with protein-level assays.

## Open questions

- How stable are translatomic states across fixation, probe sets, and tissue classes?
- Which spatial translation differences produce measurable protein or functional changes?
