# BACH1

## Type

- Human chromosome 21–encoded transcription factor and candidate developmental regulator in the Ts21 neocortex study

## Definition in This Collection

- In the developing Down syndrome neocortex multiome study, `BACH1` is a chromosome-21-encoded TF whose inferred eRegulon is up-regulated in newborn excitatory neurons committed toward an intratelencephalic lineage.
- The source links the inferred BACH1 program to pro-intratelencephalic TFs `CUX2` and `BHLHE22`, and reports that BACH1 peak expression precedes `CUX2` along pseudotime.

## Evidence and Scope

- The association is supported by paired RNA–ATAC data, differential motif/accessibility and eRegulon activity, plus recurrence in differentiated primary human neural progenitor cells.
- This page records BACH1 as a computationally nominated mechanism in this specific source. It is not evidence that BACH1 directly regulates each listed target, is sufficient for the Ts21 phenotype, or is a validated therapeutic target.
